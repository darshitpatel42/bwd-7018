Subject Details

Physics

Physics is the study of matter, energy, force, and motion.

Chemistry

Chemistry deals with the composition, properties, and reactions of substances.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms and life processes.

Accounts

Accounts focuses on recording and managing financial transactions.

Economics

Economics studies production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services.

Business Studies

Business Studies covers business management, marketing, and entrepreneurship.

History

History is the study of past events and civilizations.

Geography

Geography studies the Earth's landscapes, environments, and people.

Political Science

Political Science examines governments, political systems, and public policies.
